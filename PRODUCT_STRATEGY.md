## 📦 **MODULAR PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE**

### **CORE MODULES** (Revenue Streams)

#### 1️⃣ **MOBILE FIRST** (`@rvrfc/mobile`)
- **Purpose**: Marketing & fan engagement on-the-go
- **Features**: Hero-driven UX, match updates, trial bookings
- **Price**: €29/month
- **Target**: Clubs wanting modern mobile presence

#### 2️⃣ **DESKTOP PROFESSIONAL** (`@rvrfc/desktop`) 
- **Purpose**: Full club website & administration
- **Features**: Complete content management, team pages, news
- **Price**: €49/month
- **Target**: Established clubs needing comprehensive web presence

#### 3️⃣ **MATCHDAY LIVE** (`@rvrfc/matchday`)
- **Purpose**: Real-time match experience for fans
- **Features**: Live scores, lineup tracking, match reporting
- **Price**: €19/month
- **Target**: Clubs wanting to engage fans during matches

#### 4️⃣ **MATCH TRACKER PRO** (`@rvrfc/tracker`)
- **Purpose**: Professional coaching & analytics platform
- **Features**: Performance tracking, player stats, tactical analysis
- **Price**: €99/month
- **Target**: Semi-professional clubs, academies, coaches

#### 5️⃣ **BOOT ROOM** (`@rvrfc/bootroom`)
- **Purpose**: Internal club management & communication
- **Features**: Coach tools, player management, training schedules
- **Price**: €39/month
- **Target**: Clubs needing internal organization tools

#### 6️⃣ **RECRUITMENT HUB** (`@rvrfc/recruitment`)
- **Purpose**: Player scouting & recruitment management
- **Features**: Player databases, trial management, talent tracking
- **Price**: €149/month
- **Target**: Academies, professional clubs

## 🛠️ **TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE**

### **Modular Framework**
```
@rvrfc/core          // Shared components, auth, data
├── @rvrfc/mobile    // Mobile-first experience  
├── @rvrfc/desktop   // Full website platform
├── @rvrfc/matchday  // Live match experience
├── @rvrfc/tracker   // Analytics & coaching
├── @rvrfc/bootroom  // Internal management
└── @rvrfc/recruitment // Scouting platform
```

### **Technology Stack**
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis
- **Mobile**: Progressive Web App (PWA)
- **Payments**: Stripe subscriptions
- **Analytics**: Custom dashboard + third-party integrations
- **Deployment**: Vercel/AWS with CDN
